# Asset credits

All third-party assets below are free/open-licence, sourced from
[OpenGameArt.org](https://opengameart.org). Files were re-cropped and/or
resized from the originals (trimmed transparent padding, scaled to a common
sprite height) to keep the shipped payload small; none of the pixel art was
redrawn or altered otherwise. None of this game's art, music, or characters
are taken from SEGA's *Wonder Boy* --- see `Plan.md` and `CLAUDE.md` for the
"mood, not the IP" rule that governs the design.

## Everything else

The boomerang, gun icon, HUD hearts, and projectile shapes are drawn directly
on the canvas (no image asset) --- simple enough that sourcing one would have
cost more clarity than it bought.
